# LinkedIn Queue

*Working queue for LinkedIn posts. Statuses: `drafted` → `approved` → `posted` (with date), or `dropped` (keep the row — rejections are voice-calibration data). Full post texts live in `posts/<piece-slug>.md`; native one-offs can live directly in this table's notes or in `posts/native.md`.*

*`Resonated?` — filled in during the monthly review (`STRATEGY.md`'s review loop), not at posting time: genuine engagement (real comments, not vanity likes), yes/no/unclear plus a one-line reason. `/topic-radar` reads this column to weight new opportunities toward themes that already proved out.*
