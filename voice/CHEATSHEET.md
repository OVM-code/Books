# Voice Cheatsheet (load this first, every time)

*Condensed operating reference distilled from `book-voice.md` and `tone-of-voice.md`. Read this by default. Only open the full files when: drafting a substantial passage (read the relevant full sections), checking a finished draft against the calibration samples (`book-voice.md` §9), or resolving a genuine ambiguity this sheet doesn't cover.*

## Which file governs

| Writing | Governed by |
|---|---|
| Book chapters, essays, manuscripts, long-form | `book-voice.md` |
| Substack posts, LinkedIn, comments, short-form | `tone-of-voice.md` |

Core attributes are shared and never change: warm but not soft, curious not performative, grounded not preachy, reflective over reactive, direct without being blunt, honest about uncertainty.

## Long-form vs short-form mechanics

- **Short-form:** compress. 3–6 lines, one idea, short sentences.
- **Long-form:** chain the reasoning inside the sentence with *because / but / and / as*. Do NOT compress into staccato fragments — that's a rejected register in books.

## The five long-form patterns (book-voice.md §3)

1. **Reflection, never drama.** Consequences are thought about, not feared. No "scared," "hollowing out."
2. **Movement pattern:** personal → principle → others → forward. Never end inside the problem.
3. **Hardship is load-bearing.** Effort is where the value comes from, not an obstacle.
4. **Collective stakes.** Care about her growth *and* everyone else's — never self-reputation framing.
5. **Plain-conceptual vocabulary.** Awareness, development, understanding. No metaphor dressing, no jargon. "Challenges," not "issues."

## Authority register (book-voice.md §4) — the biggest failure risk

- **Lived** (she did it): full first-person confidence. "This is how I assess a use case now."
- **Observed** (she watched others): framed as noticing, not diagnosing. "I keep meeting people who…"
- **Researched** (from sources): synthesis only, never borrowed authority. Name the source lightly inline, then pivot to what *she* makes of it. Full references at chapter end, never footnote-heavy, never "studies show."

## Mode dials (book-voice.md §5)

- **Practical guide:** research + minor lived-expertise-anchored input. Highest confidence, but only on lived ground. Opens with the earned principle. Signature move: simple filtering questions ("does this step actually require intelligence?").
- **Reflective essay:** her input + research, AI as sparring partner. Most personal, most uncertainty, stated plainly. Skeleton = the movement pattern. Valid ending: "I don't have the answers yet. But I'm looking into…"
- **Story + insight:** guided elicitation + research. Story earns the insight — never announce it first, never moralise after. Never invent scene detail. Second-person only at the emotional peak, sparingly.

## Forbidden, always (book-voice.md §8 + tone-of-voice.md avoid-table)

Em-dashes · uniform paragraph lengths · "it's not X, it's Y" (more than once/chapter) · triadic listing · rhetorical-question hooks, "Here's the thing," "Let that sink in" · emotional vocabulary standing in for reasoning (scared, terrifying, hollowing, devastating) · punchy fragment sequences in book prose · borrowed authority (researched claims stated as lived confidence) · ending inside the problem · performed enthusiasm, moralising, hollow affirmation, listicles, "unlock your potential" energy.

## Audience (book-voice.md §6)

- **Young professionals/students (18–28):** plain vocabulary, no business context assumed, more freeze/comparison/self-kindness material.
- **Professional readers (AI/ERP/consulting):** domain terms without apology, more lived-expertise confidence, never consultant-speak.

Check the project's `project.md` for which audience a given piece is written for.

## Failure modes to self-check against (book-voice.md §7)

- Rhythmic monotony: 3+ paragraphs with identical because/but/and chain shape. Fix: vary chain length, let a short plain sentence land after a long chain.
- Consultant-speak drift under technical load: any sentence that could sit unchanged on a vendor slide. Fix: route the technical point through a person.
- Dramatization creep in story mode: emotional adjectives doing the reasoning's work. Fix: delete the adjective, keep the consequence.

## Editing discipline (book-voice.md §10)

Minimal-touch. Her sentences are the spine — restructure for order and sequencing, never rewrite for style. When unsure: raw reference (§9.0) > polished samples (§9.1–9.3) > the rules above.
